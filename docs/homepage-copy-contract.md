# Homepage Copy Contract

## Standard
Homepage copy must read like local service communication, not internal workflow language.

## Banned Public Terms
- `lane`
- `task lane`
- `router`
- `proof`
- `secondary route links`

## Allowed Exceptions
- Internal variable names.
- Analytics event names.
- Comments and non-rendered implementation details.

## Public Voice Rules
- Prefer direct verbs: `Call`, `Request`, `Schedule`, `Get help`.
- Keep CTA labels short and plain-language.
- Keep emergency wording clear and dominant.
- Preserve verified business facts and avoid invented claims.

## Canonical Source Files
- `src/content/home.ts`
- `src/app/page.tsx`
- `src/components/home/LaneTaskCard.tsx`
- `src/components/site/Footer.tsx`
- `src/content/navigation.ts`
