# Staff Training Checklist

## Summary
Concise office onboarding checklist for phone-first dispatch workflows.

## Checklist
- [ ] Access admin workspace with owner/ops authentication.
- [ ] Understand lane taxonomy: general, septic-service, evaluation, rental, commercial-service.
- [ ] Practice lifecycle updates and internal-note standards.
- [ ] Practice handling delivery/routing failure escalation.
- [ ] Practice public-facts drift escalation procedure.
- [ ] Review all lane SOPs and sign off with owner.

## Escalation Contacts
- Owner on-call
- Ops responder
- Runtime/security responder
