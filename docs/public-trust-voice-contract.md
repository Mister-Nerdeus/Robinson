# Public Trust Voice Contract

## Goal
Keep trust claims rigorously governed while presenting customer-facing language.

## Public voice rules
- Speak in plain customer language, not governance/audit terms.
- Keep confidence warm and specific.
- Preserve only `confirmed` trust claims in canonical customer copy.
- Keep provisional or blocked claims out of homepage/footer trust surfaces.

## Internal governance remains
- Claim status authority remains in `docs/claim-registry.md`.
- Evidence linkage remains in `docs/content-proof-map.md`.

## Scope
- `src/content/home.ts`
- `src/content/trust.ts`
- `src/config/company.ts`
