# Lane Conversion Contract

## Success-State Requirements
Each lane returns distinct confirmation copy and lane-specific next-step guidance.

## Quote-Shaping Guidance
- Septic lane: tank count, lid exposure, urgency.
- Evaluation lane: deadline pressure and 1-3 business day follow-up target.
- Rental lane: unit count and rental duration.
- Commercial lane: facility/service context and urgency.

## UX Surface
`SubmissionSuccessPanel` is the canonical success-state component for lane-specific confirmation behavior.
